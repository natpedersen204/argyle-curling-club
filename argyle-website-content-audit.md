### Our story

**Eyebrow:** `History`

**Section heading:** `Our story`

**Body (always visible):**
`The Argyle Curling Club began as a local effort to create a place for winter recreation, socializing, and community connection.`

`The first rink was built in 1939, with volunteer labour and community fundraising, and it quickly became a gathering place for local families and curlers.`

`Over time, the club grew into the modern facility that serves the community today, with three sheets of ice, a clubhouse, and a long tradition of welcoming both members and visitors.`

**Expandable section — "View the full historical story":**
`The club grew out of community gatherings at the Brant-Argyle School, where dances, meetings, concerts, and checkers tournaments brought neighbours together in the 1920s.`

`By 1929, local volunteers recognized the need for a curling rink and began fundraising through raffle tickets, community donations, and public events. The first rink was built in 1939 with a great deal of volunteer help, and the opening night was a major community event.`

`In the 1970s, the need for a larger facility became clear, and fundraising efforts led to the construction of the present rink, which opened in 1979. Over the years the club has added new features, expanded its facilities, and continued to serve as a social and recreational centre for the area.`

### Frequently Asked

**Eyebrow:** `Good to Know`

**Section heading:** `Frequently Asked`

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What should I wear? | White clothing does not glow particularly well under black lights. Neon, fluorescent, and high-visibility colours create the brightest glow effect. |
| Do I need curling experience? | Not at all. Glow Curling is designed for everyone, from experienced curlers to complete beginners. The atmosphere is welcoming at all skill levels. |
| Is equipment provided? | Curlers should bring their own brooms and sliders. Clean shoes must be worn on all ice surfaces. |
| Can I wear a costume? | Absolutely. Creative costumes have become one of the highlights of Glow Curling. Many teams coordinate their outfits and costume prizes are often awarded throughout the evening. |
| Is Glow Curling family friendly? | Yes. Glow Curling has hosted league events, birthday parties, community groups, and full bonspiels. All ages are welcome. |
| How many people are on a team? | Standard curling teams have four players. Contact us if your group has different numbers and we will do our best to accommodate. |
| Can I book a private Glow event? | Yes. The club has hosted private birthday parties, corporate groups, and community events. Reach out through the Contact page to discuss availability and pricing. |

## Content Audit Observations

The following observations are noted for review. No text has been changed.

---

**1. "Contact us" CTA appears on nearly every page**
The Contact page link and phone number appear in the hero (Home), the end of About, the end of Leagues (twice — table note and card links), the end of Rentals, the end of Sponsors, and the Glow CTA. The repetition may feel appropriate for a small volunteer club, but the phrasing varies slightly each time ("Contact us", "Contact the club", "Get in Touch", "Talk to Us About Sponsorship").

**2. Home page bonspiels section duplicates the Bonspiels page**
The four bonspiel cards on the Home page are a shortened version of what appears in full on `/bonspiels`. This may be intentional as a teaser, but the dates and descriptions are not identical — the Home page uses very brief one-line descriptions while Bonspiels has fuller details.

**3. Welcome / "All are welcome" language is repeated**
Variations of "welcoming", "there is a place for you here", and "all skill levels welcome" appear on: Home hero, About (Welcome section), About (Values card), Leagues (hero description), every League card, Leagues (New to curling section), Rentals (body text), Glow FAQ, and Glow features card (Community at the Centre).

**4. The Glow page FAQ overlaps with the Glow features grid**
The "Community at the Centre" feature card and the FAQ answer to "Is Glow Curling family friendly?" cover similar ground. The features grid also describes costumes, music, and food — all of which are covered in the hero description.

**5. Facility highlights appear in two places**
The facility bullet list appears verbatim on both the About page ("Our Facility" card) and is partially repeated on the Home page rentals section and the Rentals page.

**6. Rentals page and Home rentals section overlap**
The Home page rentals card lists three bullet points (meetings, birthday parties, team socials). The Rentals page lists five amenities and five suitable events. Some content is duplicated; the Rentals page is the more complete version.

**7. Social media links are placeholders**
Facebook and Instagram icons in the footer and News page "Follow" buttons both link to `#`. Real account URLs have not been added.

**8. No email address listed anywhere on the site**
Contact page and footer both list only a phone number. The Bonspiels page references `argylecurlingclub@gmail.com` in two registration notes, but this address does not appear on the Contact page itself.

**9. News page exists but is unreachable**
`src/routes/news.tsx` is a complete page with six articles, but it is not registered in the router and not linked in the navigation. Visitors have no way to find it.

**10. Sponsors page has no logos**
All 26 sponsor entries are text-only. The code includes support for logo images, but no logo files have been added yet. The cards render the business name as plain text.

**11. About page "Read More" history section is hidden by default**
The extended historical content (1920s community gatherings through the 1979 rink opening) lives inside an expandable `<details>` element. Many visitors may not notice or click it.

**12. Home page quick links section only renders one card**
The `quickLinks` array contains one entry ("How do I join a league?") but the JSX renders `quickLinks.slice(0, 2)`, suggesting a second card may have been planned and removed without cleaning up the data structure.

**13. Glow page is not in the main navigation**
The `/glow` route exists and is linked from the Mixed Glow Bonspiel card on the Bonspiels page, but Glow Curling does not appear as a nav item in the Header. Visitors must find it via the Bonspiels page or a direct link.

**14. About page board table — Pam Crockatt appears twice**
Pam Crockatt is listed as both Treasurer and Ladies Bonspiel Rep. This may be accurate but is worth confirming.

**15. Mixed Glow Bonspiel overview is vague**
The overview reads: "A mixed event that brings together curlers for a memorable evening of friendly competition." Compared to other bonspiel descriptions, this one is the least specific — it doesn't mention Glow Curling, black lights, costumes, or any of the distinctive elements described on the Glow page.
